# Code of Conduct

Sweet DAW is an open-source project intended to support constructive collaboration across different backgrounds, skill levels, languages, and areas of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, behavior, and evidence rather than people,
- welcome good-faith questions and contributions from newcomers,
- provide technical feedback that is specific and actionable,
- respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries,
- avoid sharing another person's private information without permission,
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when reviewing or promoting a change,
- help keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or targeted intimidation,
- discriminatory or demeaning language directed at a person or group,
- sexualized harassment or unwanted sexual attention,
- deliberate disclosure of private information,
- sustained personal attacks or bad-faith disruption,
- knowingly submitting malicious code, credentials, private user data, or material without the right to redistribute it.

## Project spaces

This Code of Conduct applies to Sweet DAW repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project-managed community spaces, and direct project communication when representing the project.

## Reporting

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile or a project-maintained private contact channel when one is available.

Do not publish sensitive personal information or security details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, project security, or productive collaboration.

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and should distinguish good-faith mistakes from repeated or deliberate abuse. Serious threats, malicious activity, doxxing, or targeted harassment may result in immediate removal from project spaces.

## Scope and evolution

This is a project-specific community standard. It may be refined as the contributor community grows. Material changes to enforcement or governance should be documented in the repository.
